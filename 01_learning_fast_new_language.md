# how i do learn by my self.
1 - When I start learning a new programming language, the official documentation is my first stop. But documentation alone isn't enough for fast learning — I also use AI tools to speed up understanding and solve problems. In this repository I'll share the resources, tips, and techniques that helped me learn Rust quickly, including a roadmap, examples, and practical advice on using AI effectively.

2 - Learning fast doesn't mean sacrificing understanding. The goal is to learn as much as possible in the least time by using efficient techniques. For me the best methods are deliberate practice and teaching others—writing explanations or walking someone through a concept forces you to solidify your knowledge. I started out as a beginner at this too, and I still learn by iterating and improving. I'm not selling anything here—just sharing what worked for me. Use any parts that help you, and feel free to ignore the rest.
